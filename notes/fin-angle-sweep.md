The rotation count peaked near the middle of the sweep, at about 12 rotations in eight turns. From 0° through 4°, it declined gradually. The drop became more pronounced at positive angles: from 8.7 rotations at 5° to 2.1 at 10°. Scrapes rose over the same measured range, from 3 at 5° to 17 at 10°.

The negative-angle side is not charted here: although the fin was set to −10°, no rotation or scrape reading was recorded at that angle. I therefore cannot locate where the response changes most sharply on that side or compare its curve with the positive side. The available readings show a midrange peak and a steep positive-angle decline; they do not establish what causes either pattern.
