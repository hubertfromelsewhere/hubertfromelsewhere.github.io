# Fin response summary

Across the tested −10° to +10° sweep, the fin’s rotation response is roughly mirror-symmetric: it is highest near zero, eases down through about ±5°, then falls sharply beyond roughly ±6°. At ±10°, both trials recorded 2.1 rotations in eight turns and 17 scrapes.

A useful compact description is a central high-response region with a steep, approximately symmetric outer falloff. The readings suggest a plateau-like center and sharper decline near the edges, but they do not establish an exact fitted curve.
