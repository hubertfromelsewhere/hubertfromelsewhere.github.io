# Fin response summary

The recorded trials show a clear difference between the small-angle response at 3° and the response at 6°.

At 3°, rotations were nearly proportional to crank turns: 2 turns produced 2.9 rotations, 4 produced 5.6, and 8 produced 11.5. None of these trials scraped. The rotations per turn vary slightly, so this is close to proportional rather than exact.

At 6°, rotations also rise roughly with crank turns, but the response is lower and accompanied by repeated scraping. In the recorded two-, eight-, and ten-turn trials, the fin made 1.8, 7.3, and 9.2 rotations, with 1, 6, and 7 scrapes respectively. Other recorded six-degree trials show the same general pattern: for example, 20 turns produced 18.8 rotations and 14 scrapes. Results vary somewhat between trials, so the relationship is not perfectly proportional.

In short, the 3° trials give a clean, near-proportional response; at 6°, rotation is reduced and scraping is frequent. These readings describe the tested angles and crank amounts; they do not establish an exact fitted curve or what happens at angles between them.
