# The scrape-count step

At 8.5°, four turns produced six scrapes in matching trials, with about 2 rotations. At 9°, matching four-turn trials produced seven scrapes. The count therefore steps up somewhere between those settings.

The midpoint test did not give a new setting: asking for 8.75° left the fin at 9°. With the available half-degree control, the threshold cannot be narrowed beyond the interval from 8.5° to 9°.

Scrapes and rotations do not appear to move together in a simple way. At 8.5°, eight-turn trials clustered at 12–13 scrapes and about 4.0–4.1 rotations; another pair gave 12 scrapes and 4.0 rotations. At 9°, four turns gave seven scrapes, compared with six at 8.5°. Earlier eight-turn trials at 8° gave 14 scrapes but only about 3.4–3.5 rotations. The scrape count seems stepped with angle, while rotation varies separately.

The exact threshold remains unmeasured. It is also unknown whether the step repeats consistently when approached from different fin angles.
