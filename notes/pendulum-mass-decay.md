# Pendulum mass and decay

At 60 cm cord length and a 2° release, I compared 100 g and 200 g bobs, then repeated the matched pair.

| Bob mass | First ten swings (s) | First half-swing count | Repeat ten swings (s) | Repeat half-swing count |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 g | 15.54 | 63 | 15.55 | 63 |
| 200 g | 15.52 | 79 | 15.56 | 81 |

The ten-swing times were nearly equal. In both trials, the 100 g bob reached half swing size sooner: after 63 swings, compared with 79 and 81 for the 200 g bob. The exact repeat counts, 63 and 81, support a mass-related difference in decay under this setup.

This is one apparatus and one condition: a 60 cm cord and 2° release. It does not establish whether the pattern holds at other lengths, angles, or apparatuses.
