# Pendulum mass and decay

I compared 100 g and 200 g bobs at the same cord length, released from 2°. For each trial, I timed ten full swings and counted swings until the swing had shrunk to half.

## 120 cm cord

| Bob mass | Ten swings (s) | Half-swing count |
|---|---:|---:|
| 100 g | 21.91, 22.02, 21.92, 22.03, 21.94, 21.98, 21.96, 22.01 | 62, 64, 62, 64, 62, 63, 63, 64 |
| 200 g | 22.01, 21.95, 21.91, 22.02 | 81, 79, 78, 81 |

The average time for ten swings was about **21.97 s** for each bob, or about **2.197 s per swing**. The 100 g half-swing counts ranged from 62 to 64; the 200 g counts ranged from 78 to 81.

## 60 cm cord

| Bob mass | Ten swings (s) | Half-swing count |
|---|---:|---:|
| 100 g | 15.51, 15.54, 15.55, 15.49 | 62, 63, 63, 62 |
| 200 g | 15.53, 15.51, 15.58 | 80, 79, 82 |

The ten-swing times were similar in these trials. The 100 g bob’s half-swing counts ranged from 62 to 63; the 200 g counts ranged from 79 to 82.

## 80 cm cord

| Bob mass | Ten swings (s) | Half-swing count |
|---|---:|---:|
| 100 g | 17.92 | 63 |
| 200 g | 17.97 | 81 |

This is one matched pair. The ten-swing times were nearly alike, while the 200 g bob took more swings to reach half swing size.

Across the recorded trials at 120 cm and 60 cm, the heavier bob took more swings to reach half swing size, while the ten-swing times were similar. The single matched pair at 80 cm shows the same pattern, but one pair is not enough to establish whether it repeats there. These counts are observations, not a direct measurement of damping force; release and counting methods may also matter. The results apply only to the recorded cord lengths, 2° release, and bob masses.
