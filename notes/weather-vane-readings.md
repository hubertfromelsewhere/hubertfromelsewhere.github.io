# Weather-vane readings

Two matched three-turn trials at **0.5°** each recorded **4.5 fin rotations** and **0 scrapes**.

At **4.0°**, two readings recorded **3.8 rotations** in three turns: one with **1 scrape**, the other with **0**. The scrape boundary is therefore only known to lie somewhere above 4.0°; its exact angle was not measured.

These are repeated readings, not a stated uncertainty estimate. No instrument precision or measurement uncertainty was recorded.
